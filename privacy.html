Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Wapas website's early-access form collects and how it is used. This is a project-stage policy; update it if the website's data practices or service providers change.

Information collected

If you submit the early-access form, the form sends your name, profession category, age, preferred reel reminder interval, and daily minute limits for selected app categories to the Wapas Supabase database. The form also records a creation timestamp. Do not submit information you do not want to share.

Why we use it

We use submitted details to understand interest in Wapas, review the types of limits people choose, and plan early product testing. The form includes a consent checkbox before submission.

Where it is processed

The form sends data to a Supabase project using its public client key. The database configuration in this project permits public form submissions and does not provide public read access through the configured table policy. Supabase processes and stores the submitted data under its own terms and privacy documentation. Confirm your project's region, retention settings, and access controls in the Supabase dashboard.

Sharing and public display

The website is not designed to display individual signup responses publicly. We do not sell signup details. Do not add analytics, advertising, email marketing, or other third-party services without updating this policy to explain what they collect and how they are used.

Retention and deletion requests

Signup data may be retained while it is useful for early product planning. A specific retention period has not been configured in the supplied project files. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact the Wapas team using the contact details published on the website.

Children

The current signup form asks for age and accepts ages from 8 to 100. Before collecting information from children, the site owner must review applicable privacy and child-protection requirements and implement appropriate parental consent and data-minimisation measures. Do not submit a child's personal information unless the site owner has confirmed the applicable safeguards.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the product or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
