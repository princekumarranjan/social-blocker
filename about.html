About Wapas

Wapas is an early-stage project exploring a simple idea: people should be able to decide how much time they want to spend scrolling, and receive a reminder when they reach that boundary.

What we are building

The Wapas browser extension is designed to count supported reel and short-video pages, track time spent on supported websites while a tab is visible and focused, and show a reminder when a chosen limit is reached.

What Wapas is not

Wapas is not a medical, mental-health, or productivity treatment. It does not guarantee changes in screen habits. Browser extensions also cannot measure activity inside native mobile apps unless a separate supported integration exists.

Our approach

We want the product to be simple, transparent, and adjustable. People should be able to choose their own boundaries and review whether those boundaries are useful in their daily routine.
